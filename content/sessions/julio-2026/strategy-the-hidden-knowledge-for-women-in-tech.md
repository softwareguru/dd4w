Women in tech don’t only need talent: they need strategy. This talk translates the values from the greatest strategists in world history, their mindset, and how a few exceptional women learned to win not only a battle, but the war itself.  Led by the examples of women of their time, at a deep disadvantageous scenarios, who made it through a man’s world and navigated complex stages of power. Their examples are now more necessary than ever in real tech-industry situations: building influence, choosing battles, negotiating visibility, reading power systems, and leading without losing identity. A practical session on technical leadership, career strategy, and decision-making that will impact your vision for life.
